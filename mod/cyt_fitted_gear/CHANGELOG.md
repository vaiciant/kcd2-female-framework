# Changelog

## 1.0
First release. Everything is in one mod now instead of a bunch of separate ones.

- slim short gambeson, padded hose, arm armor, cuirasses, brigandines and mail
- long gambesons, caftans, coats, waffenrocks and habits
- hoods, coifs and collars, Hood On items included
- items swap to the fitted versions on their own after a save loads
- belts, pouches and straps keep their physics
- story, reward and DLC armor swaps too, zizka's, capons, godwins, samuels, eriks, the brunswick set, the monastery habits and a few others
